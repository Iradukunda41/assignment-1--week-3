About

Architecture always seemed interesting to me, but I didn’t think much about it until last month. Our class went on a field trip to a museum shaped like a giant glass dome. Inside, a guide told us how architects plan every detail, from walls to windows. I asked, “How do they decide what shape to use?” She said it depends on the purpose of the building. Inspired, we returned to school and designed our own classroom layout. My friend Ava suggested adding beanbags for reading corners, while I sketched large windows for sunlight. Afterward, we presented our ideas to the class. Some kids loved the beanbags, and others liked the windows.
